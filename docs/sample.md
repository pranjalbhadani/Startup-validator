### 1. Startup Name

**EcoTrack AI**

**Idea Description**
EcoTrack AI is a sustainability analytics platform that helps companies measure, track, and reduce their carbon footprint automatically. Many small and medium businesses struggle to calculate emissions across supply chains and daily operations. EcoTrack integrates with accounting software, logistics systems, and energy providers to estimate emissions in real time. The platform uses AI to identify the biggest sources of emissions and recommends actionable steps such as switching suppliers, optimizing shipping routes, or reducing energy consumption.

**Target Market**
Small and medium-sized businesses aiming to track ESG metrics and reduce carbon emissions.

**Revenue Model**
Subscription

### 2. Startup Name

**SkillSwap**

**Idea Description**
SkillSwap is a peer-to-peer learning marketplace where people exchange skills instead of paying for courses. Many professionals want to learn new abilities but cannot afford expensive training programs. Users list skills they can teach (e.g., coding, photography, marketing) and the skills they want to learn. The platform matches users and schedules sessions through video calls or local meetups. AI recommends learning partners and tracks skill progress through structured learning paths.

**Target Market**
Young professionals and students interested in affordable skill development.

**Revenue Model**
Freemium (premium matching features and certifications)

### 3. Startup Name

**FreshRoute**

**Idea Description**
FreshRoute is a logistics optimization platform for grocery stores and restaurants that reduces food waste by predicting demand and optimizing delivery routes. The system analyzes historical sales, weather, holidays, and local events to forecast product demand. It then automatically adjusts ordering quantities and delivery schedules to prevent overstocking and spoilage. Businesses receive alerts and suggestions to discount or redistribute items before they expire.

**Target Market**
Local grocery stores, supermarkets, and restaurant chains.

**Revenue Model**
Subscription + transaction fee for logistics optimization services
